Featured build

ISL Sign Language Translator

Indian Sign Language is spoken with more than the hands. In ISL, facial expressions are not just emotional cues; they can change the actual meaning of a sentence. This translator reads both through an ordinary laptop webcam, then speaks the result aloud in natural English, in real time.

  1. See

    OpenCV captures live video from a standard laptop webcam.

  2. Read the hands

    MediaPipe tracks 21 landmark points on the hand and matches finger positions against a dictionary of 20 ISL words, such as I, YOU, HELLO, HELP and LOVE.

  3. Read the face

    Face Mesh follows 468 facial landmarks, measuring eyebrows, eyes and mouth to classify the emotion: happy, sad, angry, surprised, disgusted or neutral.

  4. Understand

    A local language model, run through Ollama, treats the signs as noisy input, ignores likely misdetections and repetitions, and grasps the intent behind them.

  5. Speak

    The model writes a natural first-person sentence with the right emotion, and gTTS speaks it aloud through the speakers.

  • 21hand landmarks tracked
  • 468facial landmarks tracked
  • 20ISL words recognised
  • 6emotions detected

Free, by design

Paid cloud services were planned for translation and voice, but billing and account limits led me to replace them with free tools: Ollama for language and gTTS for speech. The finished application runs at zero ongoing cost.

Modular by nature

Hand tracking, face tracking, sign recognition, AI translation and voice output live in separate modules, so each part is easy to understand and improve on its own.

Chosen with care

It runs on Python 3.11 inside a dedicated virtual environment, because core libraries such as MediaPipe do not yet support newer versions of Python. The interface is a Streamlit app with a dark-blue glass design and live emotion indicators.

Robotics & electronics

Software has never been enough for me. I like to give code a body: machines that move, sense and respond to the world around them.

Arduino Uno

The heart of every robot I have built. Dependable and wonderfully forgiving to learn on, it commands my motors, sensors and drivers.

Arduino Nano

The same spirit in a smaller body, ideal when a build needs to stay compact and tidy.

ESP32 & ESP32-CAM

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and real processing power on one board. With the camera version, my machines gain eyes and a voice on the network, like the Mars rover’s live stream.
